# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Data Officers, Government Officials, and Citizens reviewing open data quality compliance (ISO/IEC 25012) for the state of Nuevo León.

## Product Purpose
Automatically evaluate and visualize the quality of open datasets to enforce governance standards, highlight critical failures, and provide actionable recommendations to institutions.

## Brand Personality
Institutional, precise, authoritative, and objective.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, playful interfaces, loud marketing landing pages, and cluttered "metric dumps" without clear narrative or hierarchy.

## Design Principles
1. **Data as narrative:** Use visualization to tell a clear story of compliance and gaps, not just to display numbers.
2. **Objective severity:** Escalate issues based on precise ISO dimensions, using color and typography to signal urgency without panic.
3. **Clarity over decoration:** Remove any UI element that doesn't aid in understanding the data or the required action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. High contrast required for data visualizations and critical alerts. Accessible to screen readers for table and chart summaries.
